FORM & FIELD is a fictional concept shop built as a learning project. It demonstrates multiple pages, product cards, filters, a local shopping bag and a mock checkout.

What works in this demo?

No real orders or payments are processed. Connect a secure backend and payment provider before using a store commercially.
